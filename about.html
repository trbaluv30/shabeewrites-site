The Author

Shree Shabitha
Balu Vignesh

ShaBee is a passionate author who recently discovered her love for inking her imagination. Drawn to romance, she delights in weaving heartfelt tales of two broken souls navigating the journey to love and healing.

ShaBee firmly believes that everyone deserves a second chance at happiness — a belief that resonates through every story she creates.

When not immersed in storytelling, ShaBee is a full-time public health physician, working to elevate the quality of healthcare with her expertise and dedication.

The Heart Behind the Stories

Healing Through Love

Every story ShaBee writes is rooted in the belief that love can mend what life has broken. Her characters don't just fall in love — they grow, heal, and become whole.

Second Chances

ShaBee believes deeply that no heart is beyond redemption. Her novels give characters — and readers — permission to try again, to hope again, to love again.

Authentic Voices

As a physician who writes, ShaBee brings a unique depth to human emotion. Her characters feel real because they're built from a place of genuine empathy and care.
